# The Printer Finally Answers

One line, in the office printer's own voice — weary, literal, faintly passive-aggressive — as its comeback to the jams, the error codes and the thumps.

> I am not broken. I am refusing to print your 87-page email thread twice.

`main.py` writes that line (72 characters) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
